Build a per-module checklist before research. Record queries, source URLs, access outcomes, actual changes and unresolved claims. Reuse research across modules but update separate company summaries and relation records where supported; shared people arrays alone do not satisfy all modules.

Use three dates separately: `checkedAt` for attempted research, `verifiedAt` for a claim supported by read evidence, and `updateBatch` only for a material addition/correction. An inaccessible page, login shell, CAPTCHA, homepage or search snippet cannot verify a person's current employment. Do not convert old content into new evidence by prepending today's date. An unchanged claim can be reverified only when the actual supporting content has been read.

Publish a module-by-module report with record counts and remaining gaps, plus a channel coverage report. If a module is incomplete, continue available research and disclose its remaining scope; never claim comprehensive current truth from a few updated people. Conflicting reports stay unresolved in both person and company views. A correction of stale or overstated evidence is a legitimate material change.

Validate old/new sorting preferences, all script syntax, cards/table/graph consistency, current-batch badges, mirror equality, and preservation of the annotation bootstrap/export. Verify remote refs and live content after pushing; a credential warning alone does not establish deployment status.
